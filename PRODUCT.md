# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Industry research hiring teams and researchers reviewing Xinran Zhao's research, publications, and CV. Help readers understand his agenda, find evidence in representative papers, and contact him.

## Product Purpose

A personal academic homepage supporting the current industry job search, with clear research themes, a complete publication record, and a legacy entry point.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly, direct, approachable. User confirmed a layout close to Rulin Shao's original website and the simplicity of Xinran's existing homepage. Use Akari Asai's concise research paragraphs with paper links and Yiqing Xie's job-market statement as content references.

## Anti-references

No flashy effects, decorative cards, elaborate animation, or marketing hero. Preserve the original homepage and keep full publications accessible.

## Design Principles

- Let research and accurate publication records carry the page.
- Make the current industry job search explicit.
- Explain three research directions through concrete papers.
- Keep full research and legacy views accessible.
- Preserve static hosting and existing project pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive layout, readable contrast, semantic headings, visible keyboard focus, and navigation without JavaScript. No motion needed.
